Personalized CVD Browser Assistant Test Page

This page tests the extension's Ishihara profile calibration, whole-website SVG filtering, and per-image neural recoloring.

How to Test Profile Estimation & Auto-Selection:

  1. Open the extension popup and click the Ishihara Test tab.
  2. Notice the dynamic generator with randomized numerals and live dot mosaics. You can choose 5 Plates (Quick) or 10 Plates (Thorough), or click Re-roll Numbers.
  3. Click Start Ishihara Test and answer the dynamic plates.
  4. Observe the test result displaying your estimated CVD type (e.g. Deuteranopia, Protanopia, Tritanopia).
  5. Notice the extension automatically chooses and applies your profile, then redirects back to the Personalization tab with an active highlight glow!
